📖 How to Use This Tool (For Everyone)

1. Sender (You): Type your secret message in the Encrypt tab. Create a strong password — this is the key the receiver will need. Click "Encrypt Now" and copy the scrambled text that appears. Share this text and the password separately with your receiver (e.g., send the encrypted text via chat and share the password over a phone call).

2. Receiver (Them): They open this same website, go to the Decrypt tab, paste the encrypted text, enter the password you shared, and click "Decrypt". The original message appears instantly — and nobody else can see it.

🔐 Why is this needed? In today's digital world, our private conversations — financial details, personal notes, passwords, legal documents — pass through messaging apps and emails that can be intercepted, hacked, or read by unintended eyes. HudaEncryptions ensures that only the person with the correct password can unlock your message. Use it whenever you need to share something sensitive: passwords, account details, private addresses, confidential plans, or any information you wouldn't want a stranger to read. It's free, open to everyone, and works entirely on your device — giving you complete control over your privacy.
